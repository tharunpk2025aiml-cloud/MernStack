Welcome to Vice City

Grand Theft Auto: Vice City is a 2002 action-adventure game set in 1986, following Tommy Vercetti's rise to power in a Miami-inspired city after a botched drug deal. It features an 80s-themed open world, a story campaign, property ownership, vehicles, and radio stations.
